Describe your organization's work as it relates to YouTube:
---
WishFox is a web-based creator workflow platform that helps YouTube creators schedule, process, and publish short-form video clips to their own channels. Users sign in to WishFox, connect their YouTube channel via OAuth 2.0, configure posting preferences, and queue clips for automated upload.
